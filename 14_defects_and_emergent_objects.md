# Defects and Emergent Objects in HCSN

**Status:** Empirically grounded  
**Scope:** Defects, persistence, and proto-object emergence  
**Basis:** Simulation evidence only

---

## 1. Defects

### Definition — Defect Event

A **defect** occurs at time \( t \) if hierarchical closure changes discontinuously:

\[
|\Delta \Omega(t)| > \varepsilon
\]

All observed defects satisfy this criterion.
No defect occurs without a corresponding \( \Delta \Omega \).

---

## 2. Defect Charge

Defect charge is defined empirically as:
\[
\Delta Q := \Delta \Omega
\]

This is not a conserved quantity.

---

## 3. Defect Regulation

**Observational Law:**  
Defects occur preferentially during periods of geometric instability
(high variance in Ω) and suppress further instability.

Defects are **regulated instabilities**, not noise.

---

## 4. Defect Worldlines

A **defect worldline** is a temporally correlated sequence of defect events such that:
- inter-defect spacing is bounded
- local geometry remains correlated
- defect charge remains statistically bounded

Simulation data shows strong temporal clustering consistent with persistence.

---

## 5. Emergent Proto-Objects

Persistent defect worldlines form **proto-objects**:

- identity is defined by continuity, not constituents
- vertices may completely turnover
- existence depends on Ω-regime
- merging is default unless protected

Proto-objects are **emergent**, not fundamental.

---

## 6. Interaction (Minimal Definition)

Two proto-objects interact if:
> the rewrite statistics of one are measurably altered by the presence of the other.

Interaction is:
- asymmetric
- environment-mediated
- non-geometric
- non-conservative

---

## 7. Rewrite Competition

Proto-objects compete for rewrite opportunities.

Interaction strength depends on **rewrite overlap**, not spatial distance.

---

## 8. Summary

- Defects are discrete, regulated events
- Persistent defects define proto-objects
- Interaction exists but is competitive, not force-based
- No geometry or symmetry is assumed

These results are stable across parameter scans.
