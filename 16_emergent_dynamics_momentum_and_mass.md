---

\[

A region exhibits **rewrite imbalance** if rewrites occur preferentially
on one side of a worldline over time.

This imbalance is directly observable.

Momentum is defined as the **statistical persistence of rewrite imbalance**
across a finite temporal window.

Equivalent empirical measures include:
- before/after rewrite asymmetry
- variance of causal displacement
- variance of rewrite flux

These are operationally equivalent in simulation.

\[
m \sim \frac{1}{\operatorname{Var}(p)}
\]

Long-lived worldlines exhibit high mass.
Short-lived defects exhibit low mass.

Momentum is conserved **statistically**, not exactly.
