# Axioms and Methodology of HCSN

**Status:** Stable  
**Scope:** Minimal assumptions only  
**Important:** These are constraints on rewrite dynamics, not physical laws.

---

## Purpose

This document defines the **minimal axiom set** underlying
Hierarchical Closure Spacetime Networks (HCSN).

All other structures — geometry, particles, momentum, interaction —
are *derived from simulation evidence*, not assumed.

---

## Axiom 1 — Discrete Relational Substrate

The fundamental structure is a finite (but unbounded) **hypergraph**:
- vertices (events)
- hyperedges (relations)
- causal ordering relations

There is no background manifold, metric, or embedding space.

---

## Axiom 2 — Local Rewrite Dynamics

Evolution proceeds through **local rewrite rules** acting on finite subgraphs.

Each rewrite:
- is local
- modifies bounded information
- preserves causal consistency
- is probabilistically accepted or rejected

---

## Axiom 3 — Causal Consistency

No rewrite may depend on future rewrites.

Closed causal loops are forbidden:
\[
\forall v,\quad v \not\prec v
\]

This induces an emergent arrow of time.

---

## Axiom 4 — Finite Information Propagation

Each rewrite affects only a bounded neighborhood.

Consequences:
- no instantaneous global influence
- finite correlation length
- computable evolution

---

## Axiom 5 — Closure Tension Principle

Rewrite dynamics favor **hierarchical closure**
while suppressing excessive redundancy and hub dominance.

This is a *regulatory tension*, not a maximization rule.

---

## Axiom 6 — Defect Permissibility

Localized violations of closure balance are allowed.

These appear as **discrete defect events**
characterized by finite changes in hierarchical closure.

---

## Methodological Rule

Only statements supported by simulation evidence
are promoted beyond conjecture.

No correspondence to known physical theories is assumed.
