# Geometry, Dimension, Uncertainty, and Limits in HCSN

**Status:** Empirically constrained  
**Scope:** Large-scale structure and open problems

---

## 1. Geometry Is Emergent

HCSN assumes:
- no manifold
- no lattice
- no predefined dimension

Geometry emerges from rewrite statistics.

---

## 2. Dimension Is Not Coordination

Average coordination ⟨k⟩ grows monotonically,
but does not define physical dimension.

Dimension must be defined after coarse-graining.

---

## 3. Effective Dimension

Effective dimension depends on:
- hierarchical closure Ω
- redundancy density
- coarse-grained coordination

Simulations show dimensional stabilization at finite scale.

---

## 4. Uncertainty as Statistical Limit

Observed relation:
\[
\operatorname{Var}(p) \propto \tau^{-1}
\]

Long-lived defects have stable momentum.
Short-lived defects fluctuate strongly.

This is an uncertainty-like tradeoff
arising from finite rewrite statistics.

---

## 5. Known Limits

- No analytic derivation of dimensional fixed points yet
- Interactions are statistical, not exact
- Extremely long-time scaling is computationally expensive

---

## 6. Open Problems

1. Dimensional selection mechanism
2. Emergent gauge structure
3. Stability of defect species
4. Fermionic statistics
